One plan per farm and financial year (April–March). Chemical and fertiliser are costed on the full acreage. Seed is costed on the full acreage for an annual crop, and only on the acres being reseeded for a perennial crop. Average cuts is the sum of each cut’s share of the acreage — one cut of the whole crop is 1, and 100% then 80% then 50% is 2.3. Harvest cost is £ per acre, charged on the acres taken at each cut. Expected dry matter is stored with the plan. Those totals stay on this page for now — they are not copied into Financial Forecasts yet.
